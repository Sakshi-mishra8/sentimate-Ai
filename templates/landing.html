    What the AI does for you

    Reads the mood
    Positive, neutral or negative with a 0-100 score. Our own trained model, VADER and optional DistilBERT vote together.
    Finds the emotion
    Joy, anger, sadness, fear or surprise, so you know if a customer is disappointed or furious.
    Checks every topic
    "Great product but late delivery" becomes two scores: quality up, delivery down.
    Predicts who will leave
    Churn risk and a P1/P2/P3 priority for each message, built from mood, anger and warning words.
    Understands Hinglish
    Reviews like "bakwas service, delivery late" are scored correctly, not ignored.
    Groups complaints
    K-Means clustering finds the main complaint themes without any labels.
    Forecasts and alerts
    Regression predicts the next scores. Anomaly rules warn you about sudden drops.
    Writes and answers
    Reply Studio drafts responses in four tones. Ask AI answers questions about your data.
    Collects feedback
    Share one link by WhatsApp, email or your website. Every message is analyzed the moment it arrives and saved in History.
    Team inbox
    Upload a CSV and its messages land here, urgent customers first. Write a reply, send it by email, and mark it resolved.

    What happens to each review

    1. Clean and splitText is cut into clauses so mixed opinions stay separate.
    2. Three models voteVADER rules, our own trained Naive Bayes model, and DistilBERT.
    3. Emotion and topicEmotion lexicon plus aspect-based sentiment for each topic.
    4. Risk and priorityChurn points, intent detection and a P1-P3 label.
    5. Insight and replySaved to the database, then clustered, forecast and answered.
